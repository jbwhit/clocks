### Executive Summary

Revision 3 replaces the rejected heuristic and unproven constructs of Revision 2 with a mathematically rigorous design:
1. **Lattice-bounded floating-point arithmetic** guarantees absence of internal underflow.
2. **Symmetric two-boundary integer-cell decisions** with directed outward rounding eliminate midpoint certification errors.
3. **Conservative classification** mathematically guarantees coverage of every exact subnormal entry up to all supported NumPy array lengths ($N \le 2^{63}$).
4. **Isolated directed Decimal interval fallback** strictly encloses dropped tails and respects arbitrary caller context configurations.
5. **ESS call-site separation** avoids speculative DD and Decimal evaluation while provably preserving exact float64 ESS behavior.

#### 3. Soundness of the Integer-Cell Decision at Both Boundaries
- **Symmetric Two-Boundary Formulation ([`prototype.py:284-296`](file:///Users/jonathan/projects/clocks/.worktrees/normalizer/.worktrees/r3-review/docs/plans/normalizer-r3/prototype.py#L284-L296))**:
  - Initial `integer = np.rint(high)` is exact for `high <= CORRECTION_LIMIT < 2**53`. `high - integer` is exact (Sterbenz for $high < 2^{52}$; exact zero for $high \ge 2^{52}$ where floats are integers).
  - Lines 287–289 adjust `integer` by $\pm 1$ if $(high + low) - integer$ crosses $\pm 0.5$, ensuring the offset $\delta = dh + dl \in [-0.5, 0.5]$.
  - `_lower_sum(high, low, other)` computes:
    $$\text{tail} = \text{nextafter}(\text{RN}(error + low), -\infty) \le error + low$$
    $$\text{result} = \text{nextafter}(\text{RN}(first + tail), -\infty) \le first + tail \le high + low + other$$
    Because every intermediate step rounds strictly downwards towards $-\infty$, `_lower_sum` is a verified mathematical lower bound.
  - Distances to lower boundary ($integer - 0.5$) and upper boundary ($integer + 0.5$) are lower-bounded by `left` and `right`.
  - `radius = np.nextafter(EPS_Q * np.abs(high), np.inf)` rounds strictly upwards.
  - Certifying only when `left > radius` and `right > radius` guarantees that $Q \in (integer - 0.5, integer + 0.5)$ strictly. Midpoint ties or cross-cell intervals cannot pass, eliminating the round-2 lower-midpoint error.

  - Any exact subnormal weight satisfies $w_i < 2^{-1022}$, implying $\exp(d_i) < D \cdot 2^{-1022} \le 2^{-959}$, whence $d_i < -959 \ln(2) < -661.71$.
  - Monotonic rounding $\text{RN}(d_i)$ has ulp $\le 2^{-43}$ at $-661$, so $s_i = \text{RN}(d_i) \le -661.71 + 2^{-44} < -650.0$.

#### 7. ESS Split and Invariance of Observable Filtering Behavior
- In particle filtering, candidate evaluation in `_next_beta` evaluates $\text{ESS} = 1.0 / \sum w_i^2$.
- Subnormal corrections in `normalize_proto` only alter weights $w_i \le 2^{-1022} + 2^{-1052}$. In IEEE 754 binary64 arithmetic, $(w_i)^2 = 0.0$ identically ($w_i^2 \le 2^{-2044} \ll 2^{-1074}$).
- Because normal weights are bitwise identical to the branch output and subnormal weights square to exact zero, $\sum w_i^2$ is bitwise identical between `normalize_branch` and `normalize_proto`.
- Candidate bisection search is invariant under the split, preventing speculative DD and Decimal executions.
