#### 2. Near-Normal Seam Corrections & Narrowed ESS Contribution Scope
- **Correction Accepted**: The statement that *"all normal weights remain bitwise identical"* was overbroad and incorrect.
- **Evidence**:
  - Revision 3 explicitly sets `CORRECTION_LIMIT = float(2**52 + 2**20)` ([`prototype.py:31`](file:///Users/jonathan/projects/clocks/.worktrees/normalizer/.worktrees/r3-review/docs/plans/normalizer-r3/prototype.py#L31)).
  - $2^{52}$ quanta equals the smallest normal binary64 float $2^{-1022}$. Entries with $qh \in [2^{52}, 2^{52} + 2^{20}]$ quanta are mathematically *normal*, yet fall within the DD candidate correction path.
  - Because `normalize_branch` evaluates `np.exp` using libm, which is subject to standard 0.5–1 ulp rounding errors, `normalize_proto`'s DD/Decimal-certified values in this narrow near-normal strip can and do differ from `normalize_branch`.
  - The design document explicitly acknowledges this ([`2026-09-14-normalizer-r3-design.md:18-20`](file:///Users/jonathan/projects/clocks/.worktrees/normalizer/.worktrees/r3-review/docs/plans/2026-09-14-normalizer-r3-design.md#L18-L20)): *"We no longer promise bitwise identity for every entry the old floating calculation classified as normal."*
- **Narrowed Invariance Statement**:
  - Any corrected entry satisfies $w_i \le (2^{52} + 2^{20}) \cdot 2^{-1074} < 2^{-1021}$.
  - In IEEE 754 binary64 arithmetic, $(w_i)^2 < (2^{-1021})^2 = 2^{-2042} \ll 2^{-1075}$.
  - The floating-point square evaluates to `0.0` identically:
    $$\text{float}(w_i \times w_i) = 0.0$$
  - Consequently, corrections up to `CORRECTION_LIMIT` contribute identically zero to $\sum w_i^2$ in binary64. This justifies candidate evaluation in `_next_beta` using `normalize_branch`, but does **not** constitute a theorem of global SMC trajectory invariance (as tiny weights can affect resampling, log-weight updates, support, and evidence in subsequent stages).
- **SHA-256 Verification Attribution**: Acknowledged that independent SHA-256 verification was performed by the controller, not AGY.
